# Code of conduct

Participants of every background and language are welcome.

## Expected

- Be respectful and keep to the subject.
- Support historical and cultural claims with evidence, and separate fact, inference, and opinion.
- Accept constructive feedback and be patient with newcomers.

## Not accepted

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing others' private information, and submitting material you know you cannot license.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content or limit participation. Raise concerns through the contact options on the maintainer's GitHub profile; do not put sensitive details in public issues.
