## Evidence and limits

- **Verified facts:** The sources above date Nanchan Temple Main Hall to 782 and Foguang Temple East Hall to 857. Inscriptions, the scripture pillar, and modern survey history are evidence relating to the specific Foguang Temple building.
- **Modern design translation:** Deep wood, cinnabar, earth yellow/warm ivory, grain movement, horizontal span, layered support, and raking light are contemporary presentation choices. They are not historical color measurements or reconstructions of original Tang materials and appearance.
- **Scope:** Tang timber architecture includes different regions, building types, construction traditions, and later repairs. Identify the building and source when using particular historical or structural claims; do not treat Nanchan or Foguang as universal examples.

## Design use

Organize slides through material layers, spatial order, horizontal and vertical cadence, and relationships of support. Unless the supplied content discusses a specific building or component, do not use a hall, roof, bracket set, or close-up of joinery as decoration. Structural ideas should shape information hierarchy and component relationships instead of recurring as literal architectural diagrams.
