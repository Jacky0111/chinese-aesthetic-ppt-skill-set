# Guan Ware Ice Crackle: Sources and Boundaries

## Verified collection evidence

- The Palace Museum’s Song Guan ware celadon washer record describes clear, pure powder-celadon glaze. It notes that Guan ware generally has a thick glaze with crackle, and thicker glaze commonly shows larger ice-crackle cells. The record distinguishes Guan crackle from Ge ware patterns.
- The Palace Museum’s Guan ware powder-celadon vase record says powder celadon is a common Guan ware color and that many pieces are undecorated, with restrained surface treatment.
- Song Guan ware covers kiln sites associated with both Northern Song Kaifeng and Southern Song Hangzhou. Site history and institutional details remain subjects of research; this skill does not reduce all Guan ware to one site or date.

## Design interpretation

Powder celadon, thick-glaze depth, irregular large crackle, and restraint guide the visual language. Screen gradients, textured charts, and slide colors are modern interpretations, not literal reconstructions of Song design.
