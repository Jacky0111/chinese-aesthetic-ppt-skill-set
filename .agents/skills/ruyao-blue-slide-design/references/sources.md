# Ruyao Sky Blue: Sources and Boundaries

## Collection evidence

- The Palace Museum’s Ruyao sky-blue washer record describes Ruyao celadon, its gentle luminous glaze, and fine crackle.
- Ruyao chronology, kiln site interpretation, and attribution of individual objects remain topics of research. Do not generalize one collection record to every Ruyao object.

## Design interpretation

Pale sky celadon, fine crackle, soft glaze light, and deep-blue accents form a modern presentation language. Screen gradients, background textures, and typography are contemporary interpretations, not exact reconstructions of historical objects.
