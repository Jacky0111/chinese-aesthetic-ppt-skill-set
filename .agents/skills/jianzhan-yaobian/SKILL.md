# Jian Ware Yaobian

Choose page structures to fit the content and use deep black, shadowy blue, and shifting iridescence as a coherent visual language. Read `assets/README.md` first and consult relevant examples as needed. Follow `references/sources.md` for historical claims and keep collection evidence separate from modern visual interpretation.

## Visual language

- **Color:** Use near-black, charcoal, and ink blue as the ground. Bring out the iridescent spots with deep indigo, cobalt, shadowy blue, and restrained blue-violet. Silver-white belongs only in small text, fine rules, or pinpoint highlights as a modern design accent, not as a historical glaze claim.
- **Material and texture:** Suggest the depth, gloss, and changing light of a black glaze. Build identity with irregular clusters of crystalline spots in varied sizes and their surrounding graduated halos. Let the texture gather densely or disperse into open space. Avoid generic starfields, regular rings, neon-tech effects, and unsupported crackle patterns.
- **Gloss:** Reveal blue iridescence locally and shift its emphasis with the page’s focal point. Preserve large dark areas and subtle tonal depth; avoid uniform blue gradients or blown-out silver light.
- **Composition and hierarchy:** Choose title pages, explanatory diagrams, timelines, comparisons, or data components according to the information. Texture may extend across backgrounds, cards, and charts, but reduce it in dense text areas or provide a stable dark text field.
- **Typography and readability:** Restrained Song-inspired display type can set the tone, with clear contemporary type for body copy. Body text, small labels, and chart annotations need strong contrast; keep them off bright spots and shifting halos.
- **Objects:** Express the style abstractly through black glaze, crystalline spots, and iridescence by default. Add a Jian bowl only when the content calls for it.
