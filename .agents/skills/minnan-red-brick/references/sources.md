# Minnan Red Brick: Historical Sources and Design Boundaries

## Traceable sources

- [China Intangible Cultural Heritage Network: Minnan Traditional Residential Construction Craft](https://www.ihchina.cn/project_details/14684.html) — documents the craft's regions, cultural influences, layout and construction types, and summarizes a history beginning in the late Tang, maturing during the Song and Yuan, reaching a peak in the Ming and Qing, and reviving after reform and opening-up.
- [Fujian Department of Housing and Urban-Rural Development: The Cooling Wisdom of Minnan Houses](https://zjt.fj.gov.cn/xxgk/gzdt/sxdt/202507/t20250723_6970159.htm) — describes courtyard and hall ventilation, red-brick wall properties, and the brick-stone technique known as "out brick, in stone" in specific Wulin examples. Keep case details and expert explanations within the source's scope.
- [Journal of Chinese & Foreign Architecture: Schematic Structure of Decorative Art in Minnan Red-Brick Houses](https://zwjc.cbpt.cnki.net/portal/journal/portal/client/paper/538a9412d0852aa6e9264659f9209d3b) — studies composition patterns in Minnan red-brick house decoration; it does not establish one pattern for every period, region, or house.

## Evidence and limits

- **Verified facts:** The intangible heritage entry summarizes the craft as originating in the late Tang, maturing in the Song and Yuan, reaching a peak in the Ming and Qing, and reviving after reform and opening-up. Its stated distribution includes Minnan areas such as Xiamen, Quanzhou, and Zhangzhou, as well as Taiwanese and overseas communities of Minnan descent. Fujian's housing department describes brick-stone interweaving and environmental adaptations involving courtyards, halls, and walls.
- **Reasonable inference:** The contrast of brick red and swallowtail gray, staggered masonry rhythm, brick-stone transitions, and courtyard light and airflow can inform modern color fields, information pacing, restrained curves, and spatial layering. This is a design interpretation, not a historical rule.
- **Modern design translation:** This skill's brick-red, swallowtail-gray, and ivory balance; grain strength, color variation, curves, light, and component treatment are contemporary visual language, not historical color measurement, architectural reconstruction, or a copy of a particular building.
- **Unknown / use care:** Regions, periods, house types, material sources, and repairs vary. Research specific primary sources for claims about dates, craft details, motif meanings, original colors, or an individual building; do not infer them from style examples.

## Design use

Integrate fired-clay texture, staggered/interwoven relationships, swallowtail curves, brick-stone boundaries, and spatial light into page backgrounds, information fields, charts, and components. Unless the input discusses a specific building or component, do not show a complete Minnan house, wall, roof, or literal traditional motif. Do not mistake loose bricks, material callouts, or component illustrations for full-page design language.
