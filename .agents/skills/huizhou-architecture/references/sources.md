# Huizhou Architecture: Sources and Interpretation Boundaries

## Research sources

- [UNESCO: Ancient Villages in Southern Anhui — Xidi and Hongcun](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1002/): Describes the history, traditional village characteristics, architecture, and water systems of Xidi and Hongcun. Its dates and conservation characteristics apply to these heritage sites, not to all Huizhou architecture.
- [Xidi and Hongcun nomination dossier](https://whc.unesco.org/uploads/nominations/1002.pdf): Provides nomination materials on the heritage sites' architectural layout, residential courtyards, and skywell spaces. Claims about specific spatial functions should remain within the source's scope.
- [Shou Tao and Cheng Yuning, “Environmental Configuration: An Analysis of the Spatial-Landscape Model of Huizhou Courtyard Dwellings,” World Architecture, no. 1 (2023)](https://sjzj.cbpt.cnki.net/portal/journal/portal/client/paper/7655eb2746d606ebf79206bf59b5b2ec): Analyzes natural elements, formal structure, spatial sequence, landscape interfaces, and environmental construction in Huizhou courtyard dwellings; do not generalize beyond the study's scope.
- [China Intangible Cultural Heritage Network: Huizhou Three Carvings](https://www.ihchina.cn/project_details/11243): Introduces brick, wood, and stone carving in Huizhou and their craft and cultural background.

## Evidence, inference, and unknowns

- **Supported by sources:** Xidi and Hongcun preserve village layouts, traditional buildings, and water systems formed during specific historical periods. Huizhou Three Carvings refers to brick, wood, and stone carving crafts. These describe particular heritage sites and craft categories; they do not imply that Huizhou buildings share identical periods, forms, materials, or decoration.
- **Design interpretation:** The grayscale balance, dimensional roof-tile forms, matte plaster, stone texture, foreground-to-background layering, perspective, soft ambient light, and contact shadows are modern visual translations. They convey architectural material and spatial qualities; they are not historical color measurements, reconstructions, or direct reproductions of traditional construction methods.
- **Unknowns and limits:** The approved examples are not surveys of a specific building and cannot establish dimensions, roof construction, wall-material recipes, or historical color data. Precise claims about building dates, components, or crafts require a specific region or building case and traceable research sources.

## Use principles

Integrate space and material into the slide's information system rather than adding them as isolated roof illustrations. When the user's content discusses a particular building, courtyard, or carving craft, relevant forms may be shown purposefully with attribution. Otherwise, favor abstract volume, layering, boundaries, light, and whitespace. Do not use example images as historical evidence.
